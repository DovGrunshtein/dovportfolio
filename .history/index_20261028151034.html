Dovi Visuals is a content and marketing studio founded by Dov Grunshtein, a photographer and filmmaker. We run a brand's Instagram from the shoot to the campaign: a shoot every month, reels and posts, a consistent visual style, and Meta campaigns. Most of our clients are fashion, home, design and lifestyle brands. We have years of video production behind us, and we run brands of our own, so we know the business owner's side well.
